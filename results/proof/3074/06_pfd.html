Non-GSO: one satellite at its lowest altitude

Every elevation from 0 deg to the highest a Finnish reference point sees is scanned, at the perigee altitude, with the beam peak towards Finland. A constellation lowers its power near the horizon with a pfd mask that the IFIC does not carry, so a notice is high only when it exceeds with the satellite at 60 deg or more over Finland; an exceedance only lower down is review.
